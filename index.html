Traditional Gypsum vs Stretch Ceiling

Dekhiye kyu modern luxury homes aur offices mein Gypsum POP ki jagah Barisol Stretch Ceiling use hoti hai.

✕

Traditional Gypsum / POP

  • ⚠️ Fitting Time: 15–20 din lagte hain, bohot zyada dust aur gandagi hoti hai.
  • ⚠️ Water Damage: Paani ya moisture se ceiling toot ya peel ho jati hai.
  • ⚠️ Cracks & Repaint: Kuch saalon baad hairline cracks aane lagte hain.
  • ⚠️ Lighting: Sirf bahar se COB lights lag sakti hain, poori ceiling glow nahi karti.
